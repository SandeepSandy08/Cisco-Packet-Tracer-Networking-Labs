en
config t

exit

show vlan brief

Connect the PCs to the respective switch ports.

VLAN 10:
PCs connected to Fa0/1 and Fa0/2

VLAN 20:
PCs connected to Fa0/3 and Fa0/4

Test connectivity using ping.

Same VLAN → Ping successful
Different VLAN → Ping unsuccessful
